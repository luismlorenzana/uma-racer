Uma Racer: the career guide

How the game fits together and how to take an Uma from her first unlock to a finished URA Finale career. Every number here is one of the game's own rules, as released on 2026-11-05.

What Uma Racer is now

A racing game where each Uma's career is a run of short mini-games. You unlock Umas and support cards with tickets, then take one Uma through three school years. Each season you play a stage, spend what it paid on her training tree, and race her objectives. Win the URA Finals and her racing build is kept for good, and the tickets you earn unlock the next Uma. There is no clock: nothing runs on timers, nothing refills overnight, and nothing limits how much you play.

Getting started

The Hub has two pages. Town Hall holds the Stable, the Card Library and Account. Career holds your careers and the two games. The raised Career button in the bottom bar opens the season of the career you started last.

Unlock Umas: the Stable

  1. Tap the Uma Tickets chip on the top bar. It opens the Stable's Locked tab.
  2. Tap her face. The sheet shows her aptitudes (surface, distance, running style) and her growth rates.
  3. Unlock · 1 Uma Ticket. Then Her profile, where her career starts.

Unlocking is final: nothing is locked again and there is no refund, except for cards from later scenarios unlocked before this rule (below). A completed career earns another Uma Ticket.

Unlock cards: the Card Library

  1. Tap the Card Tickets chip. Filter by the seven card types and the eight focus marks (four running styles, four distances).
  2. Open a card. Its page shows its era ("First usable in …", and whether URA Finale careers take it), its power against its type's median, its effects at level 50 and its skills.
  3. Unlock · 1 Card Ticket. Every card counts at its top level, and cards are shared by all your Umas.

Only the cards of an open scenario unlock. This release opens URA Finale, so a later card shows Opens with its scenario instead of Unlock, after the unlockable ones on the Locked tab. If later cards were unlocked before, a banner at the top returns them all for their Card Tickets with one Return.

What to pick first

  • Cards URA Finale can use. A career takes only cards of its scenario's era or older, and this release runs URA Finale careers only. Later cards wait, locked, until their scenarios open.
  • A varied deck of six, with Speed in it. Each card trains at its own type's branch. The Speed branch is where most of her Speed comes from; the Guts and Wit branches add only a little.
  • Friend and Group cards train at Wit and never reach friendship, but they are wild in both mini-games.
  • Umas whose aptitudes you like. Her best surface and distances choose her objectives and the Finals' course. Her growth rates add to every session of that stat.

Start a career

  1. From her profile: Start career · URA Finale. Or from Careers → Start a career: pick her, read the six objectives she will get, then Start career · URA Finale.
  2. Open Upgrade → Deck and pick six cards. Each posts to its own type's branch.
  3. Back on her career page, the season card lights the next step.

Each Uma runs one career at a time and one URA Finale career in all; several Umas can run careers side by side.

Play: the mini-games

A season's Play is Furlong Hand, the card race on the course of her season's objective (else her next one's), with Another game: Training Yard just under it. Every game pays the same, so play whichever you like; either one checks the step. The header of her career page has Play again for both games at any time: a replay pays like a first play and never moves the calendar.

Training Yard merge · the other game

  • A 6 × 6 yard and 60 placements. Tap an empty cell to set the piece in hand; the next two are shown.
  • Three touching pieces of one colour and frame merge one frame up, on the cell just placed. Cascades resolve.
  • Hold puts one piece aside; later holds swap it with the hand. One hold a placement.
  • Every piece set down pays 3 drills; every merge pays the frame it makes. Three touching rainbows clear for a Masterclass.
  • Partners are her deck cards: set down, one completes the best merge of its colour there and lifts it one more frame (two at bond 80). Friend and Group Partners are wild.
  • Fatigue (8% of pieces) drifts toward her best piece. Boxed in, it turns to Rest; three touching Rests clear, with the Fatigue around them.
  • Grade A at 222 drills scored. Tasks for S: gold in two colours, no Fatigue left, every Partner in a merge.
  • Its drills split by the colours you scored. Rests pay nothing.

Tips

  • Merge what you want to buy. The pay follows the colours you scored, so a yard of Stamina pays Stamina drills.
  • Save Partners for a pair. Beside two plain pieces of its colour a Partner makes silver at once, gold when bonded. The higher the pair, the bigger the jump.
  • Use Hold to keep a Partner until its spot is ready.
  • Box Fatigue in early. It walks toward your best piece; trapped, it becomes Rest, and three Rests clear it for the No Fatigue task.
  • Aim the third piece. The merge lands where you place it, so place it next to the next pair up to chain a cascade.

Furlong Hand card race · the main game

  • A card race on her objective's real course: Sprint 3, Mile 4, Medium 5 or Long 6 legs, then the spurt. Each leg is a straight, a corner or a climb.
  • Before each spread, pick a line: Rail (+1 to the leg's colour: Speed on a straight, Power on a corner or climb), Middle, or Outside (Speed +1, costs 1 Breath).
  • A spread is four columns three deep (four deep in the spurt). Play a front card of the lead's colour or exactly one higher (6 is followed by 1).
  • Tap to run a card for ground; swipe down to bank it for +1 Drive. A chain of four: +1 Drive. A cleared spread: +2 Drive.
  • Stuck? Flip a draw card for −1 Breath. At 0 Breath, Speed and Power yields halve. One free redraw a spread, before you play.
  • End each leg in her style's band (Front 1–2, Pace 2–4, Late 4–7, End 6–9): +1 Drive; outside it, −1 Breath.
  • The spurt runs every card × (12 + Drive) ÷ 12. Every rival's next move is shown: surge, hold, fade or closes the rail.
  • 1st is A. It never changes the real race, and a loss still pays.
  • Its drills split by the colours you ran. Friend and Group cards count for the season's focus; a banked card counts for nothing.
3→ 4→ 4→ 5 one higher, same value, one higher: a chain of four

Tips

  • Redraw a poor spread before touching it: the free redraw is only offered before the first card or flip.
  • Bank to stay in band. When running a card would carry her past her band's places, bank it instead: Drive now, a bigger spurt later.
  • Read the terrain. Rail on a straight lifts Speed cards, on a corner or climb Power cards. When a rival closes the rail, take the middle or the outside.
  • Guard Breath. Flips, the outside and legs out of band all cost it. Guts cards keep full yield at 0.
  • Play every Partner. It's a bonus task, a Partner runs a point more, and a bonded one lights the next two cards of its chain for double.

Upgrade: spending what you earned

Upgrade has four tabs: Tree · Deck · Bonds · Skills. Each tab's label counts what she can buy there now. Nothing is random, and what you see previewed is what you get.

The tree

  • 5 branches × 5 levels × 4 pips = 100 pips. A pip is one training session. Pips open in order; a level opens when the four below are bought.
  • A pip costs drills of its branch's colour: 20, 22, 24, 26, 28 by level. A full branch is 480 drills.
  • Energy Cost Reduction on the cards posted to a branch cuts its pip cost by their summed percent, rounded up. Wit Friendship Recovery takes whole drills off a Wit pip while its card is in friendship. A pip never costs less than 1 drill.
  • Two Camps open once any branch reaches level 3 (its first eight pips). Each is four level-5 sessions in the branch you pick, 112 drills before cuts. They can share a branch.
  • Every pip previews its level, cost, gain (each stat and SP) and the part her caps (1,200 a stat) would throw away, in warning ink. Buying pays exactly the preview. Buy row buys the rest of a level at once.

Deck and posting

  • Six owned SSRs whose era allows the scenario (era 1 for URA Finale). Each counts at level 50.
  • Each card trains at one branch: its own type's by default. Friend and Group cards go to Wit.
  • A branch seats five. A new card whose branch is full goes to the first with a free seat.
  • Tap a deck card for the post sheet: the five branches with their seats, or Take out of her deck.
  • Changing the deck keeps every pip; their pay is read again from the new deck.

Bonds

  • One track per Uma, per tier, per card, 0 to 100. Pour Bond into one card at a time; Pour is preselected to reach the next stop.
  • A hint every 20 bond (sooner with Hint Frequency: every 14 at 40%, every 10 at 100%): the card's next hint skill, plus any stat or SP rewards it has.
  • 80, friendship: it multiplies sessions at its own type's branch.
  • 100: its event skills are hinted, golds included.
  • What hints give stays hers for the tier, even if the card leaves her deck.

Skills

  • Her library: her own skills, plus every skill her bond tracks hinted (a hinted gold brings its white). Her unique is equipped on its own.
  • SP cap: a URA Finale career can spend 2,000 SP. Up to 64 skills.
  • A gold needs its white, and a ◎ its ○: buy the lower one first.
  • Skills that do nothing in this game's races can't be bought.

Race: objectives and the Finals

Objectives run on the full race engine with her racing build. Mini-games never change it: only what you bought does.

From the sheet to the result

  1. The race sheet: the race (grade, surface, metres, venue), her racing build as the race reads it (five stats, this race's surface and distance grades, her skills), her running style with her grade in each, her best preselected, and the field by gate.
  2. Race runs it. The playback shows the leg, the metres to go, her place and HP; 1× or 2×, and Skip.
  3. The result: her place, the objective's status, what the win paid and the field with margins.
  4. A loss offers Retry at once: the next attempt is a new race. A win Continues to her career.

She races with her stats (capped at 1,200), her aptitudes for the race, her unique at level 5 and the skills she bought; a gold races in place of its white. The same attempt is the same race on every device. Changing her style redraws the field.

A full career, step by step

From an empty Stable to the completion screen. The Decision steps are where your choices matter most.

  1. Unlock your first Umas

    Uma Tickets chip → her face → read her aptitudes and growth rates → Unlock. Her best surface and distances shape her whole career.

  2. Unlock a deck's worth of cards

    Card Tickets chip → the unlockable cards come first → compare each one's power bar → Unlock. Get at least six, of mixed types, with Speed among them.

  3. Start her career

    Her profile → Start career · URA Finale. Or Careers → Start a career → pick her → read her six objectives → Start.

  4. Set the deck

    Upgrade → Deck: six cards. Leave each at its own type's branch.

    DecisionPost own-type cards. Only a card on its own type's branch multiplies sessions through friendship.
  5. Play the first season

    Play opens Furlong Hand on the course of her first race; Another game opens the Yard (Speed focus in Junior Spring). Either pays the same. Aim for A; S pays 20% more.

  6. Buy your first pips

    Upgrade → Tree. Pick a branch, read the gain, Buy pip or Buy row.

    DecisionBuy toward her distance's key stats. The rivals are built that way: Speed weighs most at every distance, Power more at sprints and miles, and the Stamina a full spurt needs grows with the distance (about 700 at 2,000 m, 1,000 at 2,400 m).
  7. Race when a season holds an objective

    Race → the sheet → Race → watch or Skip. Lost? Retry at once; it's free.

    DecisionPick a style her aptitude suits. The sheet preselects her best by aptitude and shows her grade in each.
  8. Next season

    Once every objective in the season is won. Repeat Play, Upgrade, Race.

  9. Pour Bond from every play

    Upgrade → Bonds. Pour into one card at a time; Pour is preselected to its next stop.

    DecisionPush one own-type card to bond 80 early. Friendship raises every pip in its branch, the ones already bought included.
  10. Open the Camps

    When any branch reaches level 3, two Camps open: four level-5 sessions each, in the branch you choose.

  11. Buy skills as SP builds

    Upgrade → Skills. Hinted skills cost less; a gold needs its white first. Keep within the 2,000 SP cap.

  12. Get ready for the Senior G1s

    Rivals there are close to a trained Uma.

    DecisionReplay a game (Play again in her career's header) to fill the tree before a hard G1. Replays pay in full and never move the calendar.
  13. On to the URA Finals

    After Senior Winter. Play the prep heat on the Finals' course, upgrade, then race the Qualifier, Semifinal and Final.

  14. Career complete

    The trophy, "URA Finale complete", her frozen racing build, +1 Uma Ticket and +2 Card Tickets. Her build stays on her profile; her stages become practice.

  15. The next Uma

    Spend the tickets, start another career. Cards carry over; bond tracks start fresh with each Uma.

Strategy tips

Each one follows from the rules above.

Once through at A gets you most of the way

With a median deck, a once-through career at A buys about 60% of the tree, brings about 3 of 6 cards to friendship, earns SP for 4–6 golds with their whites, and wins most first tries: G3s 90%+, G2s 75%, G1s half the time, the Final about half.

Replays fill the tree

One replay of each season's play buys 95% or more of the tree. The tree, the bonds and the SP cap are the ceilings, not the number of plays.

Every play pays Bond

Replay either game to reach friendship sooner. Even a loss pays (3 Bond at D), and Furlong Hand never changes the real race.

Friendship reprices the past

Pips are worked out from the deck as it stands, so buy freely: when a card reaches 80, every pip in its branch, bought or not, pays more.

Point the drills' colours

In Furlong Hand, run the colours you need and bank the rest; your Friend and Group cards count for the season's focus. In the Yard, pieces come by her growth rates, the focus colour 1.5× as often: merge what you need.

Mind the caps

Every stat stops at 1,200 in URA Finale. The preview shows in warning ink what a pip would throw away: move to another branch before you waste drills.

Let hints pay for skills

Bond stops hint skills at up to 40% off, and hints from several cards add up. Pour Bond before buying a skill at full price.

Replay before the Senior G1s

Junior fields start at 15% of a trained Uma and Senior G1s near it; the Final holds rivals back only 10%. Seasons before a Senior G1 are where an extra replay matters most.

Retry is a new race

Each attempt is seeded afresh and a loss costs nothing. If a race keeps slipping away, upgrade first: Play again is always open.

Race Bonus and Energy Cost Reduction

Race Bonus multiplies the SP every objective pays, and adds Drive in Furlong Hand's spurt. Energy Cost Reduction makes every pip in its branch cheaper.
