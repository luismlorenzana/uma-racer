The three distance groups

An Uma qualifies for a group with A in one of its distances and B or better in the other, on a surface where she's A. You pick the group when her career starts. Counts are over the 272 Umas in the private pack.

Medium · Long
140Umas qualify, all on turf

Every long G1, the medium G1s around them, and three triple crowns.

  • Crowns: Triple Crown; Senior Spring and Autumn triple crowns (the "Long Triple Crown"); Grand Prix
  • Junior G1: Hopeful Stakes
Mile · Medium
127Umas: 112 on turf, 21 on dirt

The fillies' classics and the Mile Crown, with Osaka Hai and the Autumn Tenno Sho. On dirt, every dirt G1 at a mile or medium.

  • Crowns: Triple Tiara, Mile Crown; on dirt, the Dirt Crown
  • Junior G1: Hanshin Juvenile Fillies; on dirt, Zen-Nippon Junior Yushun
Sprint · Mile
47Umas: 41 on turf, 6 on dirt

The sprint and mile G1s, with the Sprinters Stakes and Mile Championship in both Classic and Senior.

  • Crowns: Sprint Crown, Mile Crown
  • Junior G1: Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes

How many groups each Uma has

Under the recommended rule. Summer Camp's aptitude points (release 4) raise a distance a grade: one step gives 130 Umas two groups.

The calendars

The Junior year's first three seasons are the training block: three plays each, no races. Her first race is the group's Junior G1. Classic and Senior then race in spring, autumn and winter, and train at the summer camps. A season holds the months of the original's calendar: Winter is December and the January–February after it.

The bundle, a play at A

110 drills · 15 Bond · 0 SP, the same for every game (swappable stages). 13 plays pay 1,430 drills, as today's 1,400 did for 61% of the tree, and 195 Bond for two presets' decks.

SP comes from racing

13–14 objectives pay 1,080–1,280 SP before Race Bonus. With the tree's sessions, a career earns about 1,800–2,070: close to URA Finale's 2,000 SP cap, about six golds with their whites.

What the sim holds

  • Median deck: G3 ≥ 90%, G2 ≥ 75%, G1 ≥ 50% first tries, and G1 40–80% in each year
  • ≥ 75% of her Finale's stats at her first race; ≥ 90% in Senior
  • Tree 55–65%; each preset 2.5–3.5 cards at bond 80; SP 85–105% of the cap

Distance presets

One preset per distance in her group. A race uses the preset of its distance, and so does a stage preparing that race. The tree is shared, but a pip's gain is priced from the cards posted at its branch, so each preset reads its own stats from the same pips.

What her presets share

  • The tree: every pip and Camp, bought once with the drills
  • Bond: the wallet's, and every card's one bond track (outlined cards sit in both decks)
  • Skills: bought once from one SP pool; every skill races everywhere, a distance skill fires only at its distance
  • SP earned: the better preset's, as a deck swap already allows
  • At the URA Finals: one racing build frozen per preset

Example decks, no real cards. Switching is automatic by the race's distance, as the old game's presets did (D254). Upgrade opens on her next race's preset, with a switch at the top.

Tickets

Crowns and the URA Finals pay the tickets; every other win pays SP. URA Finale's era holds 52 SSRs. A ticket per objective would pay 15–16 a career and empty the era in two careers.

The pace, for a 25-Uma roster

Umas: 7 unlocked and 13 tickets in hand make 20; one ticket a career reaches 25 after 5 careers.

Era-1 cards: after T161's return, 7 owned and about 18 tickets; the other 27 of the 52 come in about 6 careers at about 5 a career.

Later: Team Showdown's Shards (release 2) add about one Uma per two careers' plays; Fan Festival's Scout points (release 3) about one card or refit a career. Tickets stay racing's reward.

Cards by scenario

The Card Library unlocks a card only once its era's scenario is open: URA Finale's 52 until Unity Cup opens in release 2. A later card shows Opens with Unity Cup in place of Unlock, and the deck still refuses a later era.

The one-time returnThe owner holds 6 cards from eras 2–5. A Library banner returns them for 6 Card Tickets (T161's return-later-cards), the only exception to "unlocking is final". None is in a deck or holds a bond track.
