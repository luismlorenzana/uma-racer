# 01 · The game loop

> **Status: generation 3, being built (M036, D380).** The career's rules are built (T154, D383), on the build's
> (T153, D382). The stages are T155's and T156's, the shell T157's and the career screens T158's. The design is
> [the drawing board v3](../research/drawing-board/README.md).

The game has no clock. Nothing runs on timers, nothing refills overnight, and nothing limits how much you play: the
loop moves when you play it.

## 1. The loop

1. **Unlock** Umas and support cards with tickets (02 · Unlocks, T157). A new account starts with 20 Uma Tickets and
   24 Card Tickets.
2. **Start a career** with an Uma ([03](03-careers.md) §1). Her objectives are frozen on a calendar of twelve seasons
   and a finale.
3. **Build a deck** of six owned cards and post them to her tree's branches ([05](05-the-build.md) §2).
4. **Each season:**
   1. **Play** its stage, 3–7 minutes, graded D to S: Furlong Hand, the prep heat on her next objective's course, in a
      season that holds an objective; Training Yard otherwise.
   2. **Upgrade** her with what it paid: pips in the tree, Bond into the deck's tracks, skills
      ([05](05-the-build.md), [06](06-cards-and-bonds.md)).
   3. **Race** the season's objectives on the race engine until each is won; retries are free
      ([03](03-careers.md) §4).
   4. **Next season.**
5. **The URA Finals:** a prep heat, then a qualifier, a semifinal and a final ([03](03-careers.md) §5).
6. **Win the final:** the career is complete, her build is frozen as her **URA Finale racing build**, and the account
   gets an Uma Ticket and two Card Tickets to unlock more ([03](03-careers.md) §6).

Every objective's win pays a Card Ticket too, so a career funds the next.

## 2. Playing again

- A season's stage can be replayed at any time. A replay pays like a first play and never moves the calendar.
- The tree, the bond tracks and the SP cap are the ceilings, not the number of plays: replaying fills them sooner
  ([12](12-pacing.md) §2).
- Practice, outside a career, pays nothing.

## 3. What a career is worth

The pace is set so a career played once through at grade A with a median deck buys about 60% of the tree, brings three
of six cards to friendship and wins most of its races at the first try, and the URA Finals final about half the time
([12](12-pacing.md)). A stronger deck, or more play, does better.
